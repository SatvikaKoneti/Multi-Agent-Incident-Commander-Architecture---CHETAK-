02. The Critical Crisis

Production Outages & SRE Pain Points

1. Alert Storms & Fatigue

5,000+ alerts flood PagerDuty & Slack within 60 seconds during a single outage. Engineers drown in false positives and redundant alarm noise.

2. Fragmented Telemetry

Critical clues are scattered across massive server logs, Datadog metric graphs, git deployment commits, and unorganized incident chat.

3. High MTTR & Revenue Bleed

Teams spend 70% of downtime figuring out *what* broke rather than fixing it. Outages cost organizations upwards of ₹24,000 / min in SLA penalties.

4. Catastrophic "Quick Fixes"

Under pressure, engineers run unverified, destructive commands (like unindexed table locks or direct reboots) that worsen the outage.
